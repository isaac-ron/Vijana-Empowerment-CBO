# Vijana Empowerment Initiative CBO — Website Project

**Project Title:** Vocational Training for Youth Empowerment
**Organization:** Vijana Empowerment Initiative CBO (registered with Dept. of Social Services)
**Target Area:** Sotik Sub-County, Bomet County, Kenya

## Background
Community-based organization founded in response to rising youth unemployment in Sotik Sub-County. Many youths complete primary/secondary education but lack further training or employment. Special focus on vulnerable groups: teenage mothers, single mothers, orphans, persons with disabilities, school leavers from low-income households.

## Vision
A society where all youths have access to quality vocational training, enabling them to achieve economic independence and contribute to Kenya's development.

## Mission
Empowering youths through practical skills training, mentorship, and entrepreneurship support to drive economic growth and social transformation.

## Training Programs (4)
1. **Vijana Fashion Forge** — Fashion and Design: tailoring, garment making, pattern making, sustainable fashion, illustration, branding, entrepreneurship, internships with local designers
2. **Glow with Vijana** — Beauty Therapy: hairdressing, styling, nail tech, makeup, spa management, salon management, customer service, partnerships with local salons
3. **Vijana Wheels** — Driving and Mechanics: practical driving, traffic rules, road safety, vehicle maintenance, basic mechanics, partnerships with driving schools/garages/transport companies
4. **Vijana Digital Hub** — Computer Training: MS Office, email, internet, digital literacy, online safety, data entry, graphic design, digital marketing, web dev/design

## Key Features
- Practical, hands-on training with experienced instructors
- Internship opportunities with local businesses
- Access to startup toolkits and equipment
- Ongoing mentorship and business support
- Life skills training (communication, financial literacy, digital marketing, time management, product branding)

## Target Beneficiaries
- School leavers (primary and secondary)
- Women and vulnerable groups
- Youths seeking self-employment skills (ages 18-35)

## Expected Outcomes
- 80% of graduates employed/self-employed within 6 months
- 50% income increase within 1 year
- 90% report improved life skills and confidence
- Establishment of sustainable, community-driven model

## Implementation Plan
- **Phase 1 (3 months):** Setup — premises, equipment, instructors, pilot Fashion program with 20–30 students
- **Phase 2 (6–12 months):** Expand all programs, intake 50–100 students, establish partnerships

## Budget (KES 4.5M total)
- Equipment & materials: 2M
- Instructor fees & training: 1.5M
- Rent & utilities (6 mo): 300K
- Marketing & outreach: 200K
- Misc/admin/contingency: 500K

## Funding Sources
Grants from donors; donations from individuals and organizations.

## Sustainability
Local business/org partnerships, subsidized training fees and services, community engagement and ownership.

## Why
This is the foundational context for all website content and copy decisions. Branding ("Vijana" = "youth" in Swahili) and program names are fixed.
