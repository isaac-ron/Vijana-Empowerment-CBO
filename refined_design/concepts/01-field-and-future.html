Born in Sotik, built for the young people who live here.

Vijana Empowerment began as a response to rising youth unemployment in Sotik Sub-County and the shortage of practical training for those most often left out: teenage mothers, orphans, school leavers, and youth living with disabilities.

Registered as a community based organisation with the Department of Social Services, we work alongside local businesses, faith groups, and government to deliver hands-on training, mentorship, and the early business support that makes a skill pay.

Our vision

A Kenya where every young person can reach a real trade, earn a living from it, and pass that independence on.

Our mission

Practical skills training, mentorship, and entrepreneurship support that move youth from training bench to paid work within months, not years.
