Born in Sotik

We started where the gap was widest.

Vijana Empowerment grew out of one observation: young people in Sotik Sub-County wanted to work, but the practical training that leads to work was out of reach, especially for those carrying the most.

Registered as a CBO with the Department of Social Services, we partner with local businesses, faith groups, and government to keep the workshop open and the trades current.

“We don't hand out certificates. We hand young people a trade, the tools to practise it, and someone who picks up the phone after graduation.”
— The team at Vijana Empowerment
