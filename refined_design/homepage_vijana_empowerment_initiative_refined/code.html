Born in Sotik, Built for Kenya

Established in the heart of Sotik Sub-County, Bomet County, Vijana Empowerment Initiative began as a grassroots response to local unemployment. We recognized that the vibrant potential of our youth was untapped due to a lack of practical industry skills.

Today, we stand as a beacon of hope, bridging the gap between education and economic independence through hands-on apprenticeship and mentorship programs.
