Our Mission in Bomet County

Empowering the Unseen Potential of Youth.

In Bomet County, youth unemployment stands as a significant barrier to development. For teenage mothers, orphans, and persons with disabilities (PWDs), these challenges are compounded by systemic neglect and limited access to vocational training.

Implementation Roadmap

Phase 1 2023 - 2024

Foundational Setup

Established community hubs in 3 Bomet sub-counties. Recruited local trainers and finalized curriculum for digital literacy and vocational trades.

Phase 2 2024 - 2025

Impact Expansion

Scaling training to 500+ participants per quarter. Launching the "Seed Fund" for micro-enterprises led by program graduates.

Phase 3 2025 Beyond

Institutional Maturity

Full integration with local government policies and establishing a permanent regional training academy.
